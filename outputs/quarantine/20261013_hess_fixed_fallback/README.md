# Quarantine 2026-10-13: the WTC refit's Hessian at the FIXED 3e-4 fallback step (and the read built on it)

**The bug.** `ccx_hessian_per_direction()` (`R/calibrate.R`) gave every direction without a 2 % curvature plateau a
FIXED step of 3e-4 in u (`CCX_HESS_FALLBACK`). Under label `hilda_csl3v0kaidgbemwtc` the objective's numerical noise
floor is about 1e-5 in logpost (`outputs/diagnostics/objective_noise_floor_20261013_wtc.log`), so that step reads
curvature noise of order 1e-5 / (3e-4)² ≈ 110.
- At id 17, the WTC refit's promoted mode, the two no-plateau directions were `kz_deep_frac` and `h_ml`.
- `kz_deep_frac`'s real curvature is ≈ −2.4 (flat to within 2.5–7 % from 3e-3 to 3e-1); at 3e-4 it read **+46.86**.
- The Hessian came out NOT negative-definite, with one eigenvalue at +9.628 on `kz_deep_frac` (−0.72) and `h_ml`
  (−0.49).
- So the WTC read had **no Laplace sd** for the two new names.

**Fixed** at `9200485` by Marcus's ruling ("Most-stable step (Recommended)", CHANGELOG 2026-10-13 c). A no-plateau
direction now takes the interior grid step whose curvature disagrees least with its neighbors.
