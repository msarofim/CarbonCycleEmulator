## 1. The design — one global loop, one volume W = `w_gthc` [Sv], seven legs
Legs, all three tracers through `add_flux`, bulk, α₁₃ = 1, ⛔ no ¹⁴C fractionation, no ENSO term (a MEAN limb):
1. **ML_NH → HS_NH** at `y[ml_NH] / (a_ml,NH · h_ml,NH(t))` — the poleward surface transport (as `w_thc` leg 1)
2. **HS_NH → HD_NH** at `y[hs_NH] / (a_hs,NH · h_hs(t))` — NADW formation (as leg 2)
3. **HD_NH → oc[nl]_NH** at `y[hd_NH] / (a_hs,NH · dz_hd(t))` — the abyss (as leg 3)
4. ⭐ **oc[nl]_NH → oc[nl]_SH** at `y[oc nl, NH] / (a_ls,NH · dz_nl)` — the deep interhemispheric limb (bottom to bottom; the bottom layer's dz does not move under `seas_ml`, by `ccx_grid_at_h_ml`'s construction)
5. **SH interior upwelling** from the bottom to the return layer `k_gthc`: `oc[j+1]_SH → oc[j]_SH` for j = k … nl−1 at `y[oc j+1, SH] / (a_ls,SH · dz_{j+1})` (empty at k = nl), then **oc[k]_SH → HD_SH** at `y[oc k, SH] / (a_ls,SH · dz_k)`, then **HD_SH → HS_SH** at `y[hd_SH] / (a_hs,SH · dz_hd(t))` — the Deacon cell: Circumpolar Deep Water enters the Southern Ocean's deep box and outcrops in the cold surface box (the leg that does the work; the reverse of `w_thc` legs 2/3)
6. **HS_SH → ML_SH** at `y[hs_SH] / (a_hs,SH · h_hs(t))` — the equatorward surface return (the reverse of leg 1)
7. ⭐ **ML_SH → ML_NH** at `y[ml_SH] / (a_ml,SH · h_ml,SH(t))` — the surface interhemispheric limb (the SH ML's thickness at t, evaluated OUTSIDE the hemisphere loop — the conveyor's `.h_up` idiom)
Volume in = out at every box by construction (one W everywhere), so a uniform column is a fixed point (P0c). Legs 1–3 and 5–6 are emitted INSIDE the hemisphere loop under the hemisphere's own `gr` / `h_hs_t` / `dz_hd_t`; legs 4 and 7 AFTER it (the conveyor's site), each over its SOURCE hemisphere's area.
**`k_gthc`** (1-based return layer in the SH column, STRUCTURAL, default nl = the bottom = the handoff's leg 5 exactly). At the canonical grid (`ccx_default_params()`: ML 75 m; interior layer tops 75 / 127 / 200 / 301 / 444 / 643 / 923 / 1314 / 1861 / 2627 m, bottom 3700 m) the CDW depth range 1–3 km is layers 8–9; the probe scans k ∈ {10, 8}.
**Deferred by name (not in this build):** a second (AABW) cell; an ENSO term; a hemispheric split of W; an NH return layer other than the bottom.
