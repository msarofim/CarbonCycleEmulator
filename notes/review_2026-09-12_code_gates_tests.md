## B. INEFFICIENCIES (measured; the objective's and the suite's)
- **B1 — ~55 % of every objective evaluation is wrapper overhead** (Rprof, warm `lp(u)` 27 ms here / 163 ms on Torch). `ccx_periodic_spinup` makes **~262–298 one-year `ccx_run_fast` calls** per evaluation (6 Newton × 37 + 2 monodromies × 37 + finals), each re-marshalling `ccx_cpp_par` (8 %), `ccx_cpp_aux` (8 %) and re-resolving substeps via `ccx_diffusion_eigmax` (7 %); `.Call` itself is 34 %. `ps_year_forcing()` rebuilds the full forcing from disk **3× per evaluation** (`ccx_build_forcing` 20 %; `read.table/scan` 12 % — `ccx_nuclear_c14_annual()` re-reads the nuclear CSV directory on every call, uncached, `R/forcing.R:297-312`); the aseasonal seed is recomputed each call (12.5 %). Fix: marshal once per solve and call `.ccx_cpp_env$ccx_run_cpp` directly in `ps_phi`; build the quiet one-year forcing once and overwrite `c14_cosmo`; cache the nuclear series (`local()` idiom); optionally a chord/Broyden Jacobian after iteration 1 (PLAUSIBLE, convergence untested). **≈2× per evaluation → a 5 h refit ≈ 2.5–3 h.**
- **B2 — `ccx_spinup` re-marshals on every derivative call** (188 `ccx_deriv_fast` per spinup, 0.19 ms each of which 0.084 is C++): ~half of its 40 ms.
- **B3 — the R `ccx_deriv` recomputes `ccx_seas_norms(p)` 4× per call** (both hemispheres' 720-point insolation + q10 means; 29 % of the seasonal R derivative; 9.4 ms vs 1.7 ms aseasonal). It is the reference core AND the app's live flux trace (`app/global.R:222`): a 1750–2024 R run ≈ 500 s at the mode (the C++ header's "26 s" is stale). Fix: compute in `ccx_prepare()` → `aux$seas_norm`.
- **B4 — the suite: 9.0 min, of which `test-parity` 151 s, `test-so-entrainment` 104 s, `test-alpha-as` 48 s = 56 %.** Redundant full runs (all CONFIRMED): `test-enso-demean` scores 8 objectives per `edm_price()` where 4 are distinct (`own`/`other` alias `periodic`/`aseasonal`), ×3 calls, the control using 2 of its 8 → ~14 evaluations saved; `test-gate-bounds` re-sources the gate lib (→ `ccx_prepare_obs()` + `lib_seas_limbs`) in 14 blocks; `test-gradient` (`gr_run` ×5), `test-mechanisms` (`mech_run` base ×5), `test-climate`/`test-pulse` (`ccx_run_coupled` ssp585 ×3, `pulse_pair(100)` ×3), `test-ocean-advection` (`run_w` ×3 each), `test-emission-seasonality`/`test-luc-nh-share` (forcing pair ×7), `test-spinup-diagnostics`/`test-gate-p7`/`test-hs-surface-rows` (duplicated fixtures). File-level memoised fixtures throughout.
- **B5 — gate reports re-run arms they already hold** (`dg_gateH`, `dg_gateS`, `dg_gateG` re-spin; `dg_streams` re-reads obs) — 0.4–0.6 s each; low value.
- **B6 — duplicated helpers**: `skip_if_no_cpp` redefined in 22 test files despite `setup.R`; the hand-typed HILDA block (`hs_frac 0.16; u_hs 38; tau_q 538; sst_hs 2`) in 7 files instead of `ccx_apply_structure(p, "hilda")`; `closed_forcing`/`test_state`, scalar-forcing builders, `quiet_forcing`, `grad_at` copied across files.
