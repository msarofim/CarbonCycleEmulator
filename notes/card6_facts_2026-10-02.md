## Three sentences that are now out of date

**F1. Touches "…so the band would have been too wide."** It has been MEASURED, and the factor is about two.
- A sampler's band is **0.41×** the Gaussian band's width for CO₂ and **0.58×** for global temperature. These are medians over all
  seven scenarios at 2100 and 2150; the ranges are 0.28–0.61× and 0.48–0.79×.
- Example, high emissions (vvH), CO₂ in 2150, 5–95 %:
  - sampled 815–837 ppm;
  - Gaussian 812–851 ppm;
  - the app's line 826.6 ppm.

**F2. Touches "…the curvature at the optimum is well-behaved enough to quote individual standard errors…"**
Not for every parameter. Compared with the curvature's own (marginal) standard errors, the sampled spreads are:
- **≈ 0.75×** along CO₂ fertilisation: `beta_max` 0.74×, `fert_k` 0.77×, `ndep_ret` 0.73×;
- **≈ 3×** for the mixed-layer depth `h_ml` (2.98×). Its centre is also shifted, by −1.3 curvature standard errors;
- **1.3–1.4×** for `kz_deep_frac` and `imb_share_ocean_rest`.

⚠ These are factor-level numbers, for three reasons:
- They are measured in the fitting (unconstrained) coordinates.
- They come from chains that had not converged: ESS 14–100 for these parameters.
- They are not natural-unit standard errors.

**F3. Touches "Running a real sampler is planned for the final model, not this beta: … no sampler has yet converged on
this posterior…"** A sampler HAS now been run.
- The run: adaptive Metropolis, 4 chains, 220,000 iterations in all (8 h per chain on NYU's Torch cluster).
- It did **not** converge along the CO₂-fertilisation direction. R-hat for the fertilisation strength is 1.21, against a reference of
  < 1.01.
- So "no sampler has yet converged" remains true, and "planned" no longer is.
- Ruling 10-02 (u): no further sampling for this beta.
