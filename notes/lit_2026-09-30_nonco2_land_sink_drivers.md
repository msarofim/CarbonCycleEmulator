## 2. Translation into CCX terms
Arithmetic inputs:
- GCB 2025 S_LAND from `data/gcb_budget.rds` (RSS-corrected; the 2015–24 mean of 2.36 matches Table 5's 2.4): **1.30 / 2.07 / 2.52 GtC/yr** for 1960–89 / 1990–2009 / 2010–23. Budget imbalance 0.57 / 0.68 / 0.40 (C).
- IGCC GMST (`data/forcing_gmst.rds`, relative to 1850–1900): 0.35 / 0.76 / 1.12 K (C).

**(a) N deposition**
- **(i) Form.** `npp_h *= (1 + γ_N·ΔNdep_h)` on land deposition above 1850 (Tg N/yr), as the last forcing column. It feeds the existing pools via the existing allocation.
  - Precedents: CNit's exp(f1·AD), which is linear at these magnitudes, and the C4MIP ε metric (N19).
  - A saturating ΔN/(1 + ΔN/N_sat) is optional (N17). Hemispheric means (~7 kg N/ha/yr) sit below site saturation.
  - Leave out the Janssens Rh channel in v1. Whole-ecosystem C-N responses already include it.
- **(ii) Prior.** Put it on r = extra NPP (kg C) per kg N deposited on **land**; then γ_N,h = r·10⁻³/NPP0_h.
  - Centre **r ≈ 6** (C: 19.4 kg C/kg N forest NPP (N9) × forest share 0.30 of land deposition; that share is 0.25–0.36 = 23.1/(63.9–92.7)).
  - Log-SD ≈ 0.9, so 2σ reaches CLM4.5's 36 (C: 1500/41.6, N16).
  - This gives **γ_N ≈ 0.8–1.0 × 10⁻⁴ per Tg N/yr** (NPP0 70–56).
  - Gate: CCX's sink per ΔNdep should fall inside 0.6–8.7 kg C/kg N (land-mean, table below).
- **(iii) Time profile.** ΔNdep above the 1860s (C, from N6): 2.8 (1910s), 18.9 (1960s), 39.7 (1980s), 44.3 (2010s) Tg N/yr. Window means 29.1 / 42.7 / 44.3.
  - Before 1850: flat. No dataset found.
  - Future: plateau or decline. NOy falls back towards pre-1980 levels while NHx rises (N3); the 2050 total is ~flat (N2). To 2300: the emissions proxy.
  - **A constant ΔNdep gives a sink that decays** as pools equilibrate. This is the property the PI wants.
- **(iv) Implied sink.** C: r_land × 10⁻³ × ΔNdep, with r_land = forest r × 0.30. Share of S_LAND in brackets.

  | case | r_land | 1960–89 | 1990–2009 | 2010–23 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | AGWB only (N8) | 0.6 | 0.02 (1 %) | 0.03 (1 %) | 0.03 (1 %) |
  | **central, forest ~13 (N10)** | 3.9 | **0.11 (9 %)** | **0.17 (8 %)** | **0.17 (7 %)** |
  | ¹⁵N ceiling (N14) | 8.7 | 0.25 (19 %) | 0.37 (18 %) | 0.39 (15 %) |
  | CLM4.5 NBP (+synergy) (N16) | 8.2 (16.6) | 0.24 (0.48) | 0.35 (0.71) | 0.36 (0.74) |

  - Extra NPP is only **0.3–0.5 %** (0.17 / 0.25 / 0.26 Pg C/yr at r = 5.8).
- **(v) Double counting.**
  - **With β:** GCB's CO2 effect (S1) includes N (G3). CCX's β absorbs N today (N20), so β must fall. The C×N synergy is automatic in the product form.
  - With q10: only if the Rh channel is added.
  - With E_LUC: cropland N buys no carbon (the land-mean r handles this). GCB2025's transient-density BK may already carry a small N effect (unquantified).
  - NOx ozone damage is out of scope.

**(b) Climate greening**
- **(i) Form.** `npp_h *= exp(σ_T,h·ΔT_h)` on annual-mean T (G9, G12), optionally minus κΔT² for a thermal optimum (G8). Keep it **off the seasonal path** (a choice for Marcus).
- **(ii) Prior.** Global **σ_T = 0 ± 0.03 K⁻¹** (C, MAGICC6-C4MIP, G9). ESMs lean negative (G5: 8/10; G12: median −0.14 per K of land T). Satellite greening from climate is small with mixed sign (G1).
  - If hemispheric: NH ≥ 0, SH ≤ 0. **This is my suggestion, not a literature number.**
- **(iii) Time profile.** Tracks ΔT. ⚠ A **positive σ raises post-2100 uptake** under high warming, the wrong direction for Koven 2022. Only a peaked or negative σ helps the future. Its historical value is that it releases β.
- **(iv) Implied sink.** ΔNPP ≈ 60·σ·ΔT (C, NPP0 = 60). At σ = 0.01 K⁻¹: 0.21 / 0.46 / 0.67 Pg C/yr in the three windows. The sink fraction is CCX-dependent, so measure it.
  - The binding target is the **net** (NPP term + q10): GCB **−0.8 ± 0.7 GtC/yr** in 2015–24, relative to 1901–20 climate (G3). CLM4.5 shows the same pattern: NPP +0.84 but NBP −1.07 (G4).
- **(v) Double counting and identifiability.**
  - σ_T and q10 are confounded: ρ = −0.89 across models (G15). MAGICC6 and OSCAR fit them **jointly on ESM factorial runs**, not on history (G9–G10). Climate and N dep are also inseparable in flux data (N17).
  - Hector's history calibration gives **q10 = 1.2 with no NPP-T term** (G11), close to CCX's 1.1. Flux-tower Q10 is 1.4 ± 0.1 (G14).
  - Identification needs the seasonal cycle, IAV (GATE V), or an ESM prior. Overlap with CO2 is small (G1: greening is 70 % CO2).

**(c) Recovery from past disturbance**
- **(c-i) 1750 deficit: not supported.**
  - Ice cores show land C stable or gaining over 755–1850 and a **release after ~1700** (R10, R11). GCB adds a 30 ± 20 GtC E_LUC **source** for 1750–1850 (R5).
  - The legacy of pre-industrial clearing is continued **emission** (R9). Larger early clearing shrinks the modern sink through E_LUC timing, not regrowth (R10).
  - So the sign at 1750 is a source, consistent with CCX's already negative 1870–1920 growth error.
- **(c-ii) Regrowth not in E_LUC: small and weakly constrained.**
  - Regrowth after abandonment, harvest and shifting cultivation **is in E_LUC** (R1, R3). The repo already holds it as GCB 2025 "forest regrowth (total)" in `data/forcing_luc_gross_split.rds`. So `f_luc_perm < 1` **double counts**.
  - What remains outside E_LUC: recovery from natural disturbance (only a net sink if the disturbance regime changed), fire suppression (US ~0.04 Pg C/yr, R6) and pre-850 demography. Pugh's ~0.6 "demography alone" cannot be split from R3 using the abstract (R7).
  - If built: a prescribed, decaying additive sink into wood with prior centre 0 and half-normal width ~0.2 Pg C/yr. **That width is my judgement.** It is scenario-independent and decays to 0.
- **(v) Double counting.**
  - ⚠ The fitted **luc_scale 0.85 already is a recovery-like sink** of 0.26 / 0.26 / 0.23 GtC/yr in the three windows (C: 0.15 × E_LUC 1.755 / 1.714 / 1.520). Any (c) term trades against it.
  - Use RSS-corrected S_LAND only (R4). Pre-2025 editions are ~19 % higher.

## 3. Bottom line for design
1. **N dep: build it** (CMIP6 CCMI 1850–2014+SSP, or CMIP7 FZJ-2-0 1850–2022; emissions proxy to 2300). Linear NPP multiplier has precedents (CNit, C4MIP ε).
2. Expected N-dep sink is **small: central 0.11–0.17 GtC/yr (7–9 % of S_LAND), range 1–19 %**. It will not remove the fertilisation excess alone.
3. Prior on **r ~ 6 kg C NPP per kg N on land (log-SD 0.9)**. Gate on the implied sink per N.
4. CCMI deposition is 30–45 % below obs: choose knowingly; consider a deposition-scale uncertainty.
5. The N-dep future (plateau, then a decaying sink) is what the PI wants. Literature: 2–10 % of the future sink (N18).
6. **Greening: the sign is unresolved.** ESM NPP-T is mostly negative, greening attribution is small and positive, and GCB's net climate effect is −0.8 ± 0.7. A positive σ worsens post-2100 uptake, so use a peaked form or a sign-free prior of 0 ± 0.03 K⁻¹.
7. σ_T and q10 are **not jointly identifiable from the historical sink**. Pin one via the seasonal cycle or IAV, or fit to the GCB net climate effect.
8. **(c-i) 1750 deficit: the evidence says no.** Land was near balance to ~1700, then a source.
9. **(c-ii):** land-use regrowth is already in GCB net E_LUC and the repo's gross split; only natural-disturbance, fire-suppression and pre-850 legacies remain, all small and poorly constrained.
10. luc_scale 0.85 is already a ~0.25 GtC/yr hidden "recovery" sink. Price (c) against it.
11. Pre-registration update: (a) helps β modestly, (b) may hurt the future, and (c) lacks receipts. The **D3 imbalance** (0.4–0.7 GtC/yr) is larger than any of the three.
12. Anything entering code needs `data-raw/` provenance (URL, version, checksum) and cited constants.
