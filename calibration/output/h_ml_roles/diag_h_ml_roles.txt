THE BINDING CONSTRAINT ON THE EXPORT ROUTE. The model is ALREADY on the
under-depleted side of this target at the mode, and buying export with
kz moves it FURTHER that way -- because diffusion ventilates 14C in
proportion to the carbon it moves. That is what a pump does NOT do: it
exports carbon as sinking particles and remineralises it at depth,
raising deep DIC without mixing surface water down. So the pump is not
merely another export knob -- it is the only one that can add uptake
WITHOUT paying this term. Item 9's case is better motivated than the
h_ml decomposition alone suggests.
