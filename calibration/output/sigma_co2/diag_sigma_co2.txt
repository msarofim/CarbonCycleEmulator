That is the margin. It is not large: a few tenths of a ppm of CO2 level

and inside the ~30 ppm structural misfit this repo has been chasing.

the mode is interior. There is no boundary in the coordinates the sampler
uses -- which is what the mixing claim was about.

=== PART 7. THE ONE ARGUMENT THAT SURVIVES: THE STREAM SETS ITS OWN VOLUME ===
parameter point                                          R_pool   effective sd
ccx_default_params()                                      1.454         1.4659
pre-gradient MAP mode's parameters (18-par, quarantined)      0.614         1.0251

As the fit improved (R 1.454 -> 0.614), the CO2 level stream got
2.04x LOUDER in variance terms (effective sd 1.4659 -> 1.0251).
Nothing in the objective asked for that; it is what a free sigma does.
Fixing sigma_co2 removes the feedback. It does NOT re-weight the stream
much at the current fit (part 5: 1.05x), but it stops the weight
moving with the residual on future refits.

THE COST OF FIXING IT, stated plainly: the free sigma is also the only
adaptive valve on the CO2 level stream. Part 2 measured the margin at the
mode as 0.789 ppm -- MCMC excursions will cross it -- and where they do,
a fixed sigma prices a bad CO2 fit at full volume with no give.
