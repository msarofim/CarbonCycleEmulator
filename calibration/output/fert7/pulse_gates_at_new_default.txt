  READ THE MAGNITUDE WITH CARE, not just the sign. The sign is the gate
  and it is decisively restored. The high-background airborne fraction the
  saturating form produces is LARGE and is NOT benchmarked against anything:
  DESIGN gate 12 and Joos et al. (2013) are both at a ~389 ppm background,
  not the ~1161 ppm ssp585 reaches. What the saturating form asserts is that
  at 7x preindustrial CO2 the land sink is close to saturated, so a pulse has
  nowhere to go but the atmosphere. That is the physics being adopted, and it
  is a MODEL CLAIM at the far end of the app's slider rather than a fitted
  result. The log form's answer there is not a safe fallback -- it says the
  same pulse nearly VANISHES in 100 years, which is worse and in the
  direction that flatters the scenario.
