  GCB 2015-2024: land 2.36, ocean 3.22 GtC/yr.
  Log beta 0.70's cumulative land uptake is 11.1 GtC; the closest MM row
  is MM K=300 bmax=0.874 (+2.0 GtC).

  BUT DO NOT SELECT ON THAT COLUMN, and this is the one trap in this file.
  Criterion (a) anchors the two forms at ONE CO2 level, so they cross there
  and disagree on both sides -- MM is MORE responsive below 388 ppm (it is
  near-linear there) and less above. `cum_land` is also not independent of
  `co2_bias`: a case that runs the atmosphere high takes up more land carbon
  because of it. THE INTEGRAL IS BOUGHT THROUGH THE CO2 RECORD, so co2_bias
  and loglik are the columns that price it, and they are the ones used to
  build the shortlist in sec.5.

  The log rows move enhancement and slope TOGETHER -- their ratio is
  fixed at chi/chi0 no matter what beta is. The MM rows hold enhancement
  CONSTANT while the slope varies 2-fold. That is the whole point, and it
  is why no value of beta could have passed both gates.

  z_land gets WORSE as luc_scale falls, while the CO2 bias explodes. The
  two levers are NOT additive at fixed fertilisation parameters -- they are
  partially REDUNDANT, because MM at luc_scale = 1 has already delivered the
  near-zero CO2 bias that luc_scale was introduced to buy. ITEM 8's JOINT
  REFIT IS WHAT DECIDES BETWEEN THEM; neither should be set by hand against
  the other, and the handoff's beta 0.55 / luc_scale 0.70 settlement does
  not carry over to this form.
